# Privacy Policy for Silcas Bot

*Last Updated: May 3, 2025*

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Silcas Bot ("we", "our", or "the bot") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Discord bot service. We are committed to ensuring the privacy and security of all users.

## Information We Collect

### User Data
- Discord User IDs
- Discord Usernames
- Discord Guild (Server) IDs
- Discord Guild Names
- Message content (only for command processing and moderation purposes)
- Attachments (only when explicitly submitted for scanning)

### Usage Data
- Command usage statistics
- Error logs
- Moderation actions (timeouts, bans, etc.)

## How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To provide and maintain our service
- To detect, prevent, and address technical issues
- To enforce server rules and moderate content
- To improve our bot's functionality
- To respond to user requests and support needs

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored securely in a local database
- We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties
- Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy

## Third-Party Services

Our bot may use the following third-party services:
- VirusTotal API (for URL scanning)
- Content moderation APIs (for media scanning)

These services receive only the specific content you submit for scanning (URLs or media) and do not receive your personal Discord information.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request access to the data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to our processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact the bot administrator through Discord.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through Discord or via email at [contact@silcasbot.com](mailto:contact@silcasbot.com).

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information based on:
- Your consent when using our bot
- The legitimate interests of server administrators to moderate their communities
- The necessity to fulfill our service to you

By using Silcas Bot, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
